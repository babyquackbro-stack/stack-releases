## Installing

1. Click **Download Stack for Windows** above and open the file when it's downloaded.
2. Windows may say **"Windows protected your PC"**. That's because Stack is made by one person, not a big company. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Stack installs and opens by itself. Make an account, or keep it on this PC only.

**Updating:** Stack checks for new versions by itself. When one is ready you'll see **Update available**. Click it, and Stack downloads the new version, restarts, and carries on. Your tasks and settings are kept.

## What Stack does

- **Tasks that sort themselves.** Everything sits in Low, Medium or High, and moves up on its own as its due date gets closer, so the next thing to do is always at the top.
- **Revision you can measure.** Revision is logged in minutes against a total, so you can see how far through each topic you are.
- **Exams.** Add your exams and topics; Stack plans revision into your free periods and shows how ready you are.
- **Your timetable.** Lessons, free periods, clubs and events in one place, with homework fitted into the gaps.
- **Teams homework.** Optional: brings in your assignments from Microsoft Teams.
- **A bar along the top of your screen** with today's timetable and your High tasks.

## Game mode

Switch it on in **Settings → Extras** and Stack becomes a logbook you turn the pages of:

- **XP and levels** for the work you finish, and **ink** to spend in the shop on new inks, covers and pages.
- **Seals, finds and achievements**, with a streak for school days in a row.
- **Practice papers become boss fights** against the clock, and finish with a stick-figure fight: how it goes depends on your grade (score 100% for a special one).
- Switch it off any time and normal Stack is exactly as it was.

## On your phone

Stack also works on iPhone and Android: open **[stack-planner-a0b8c.web.app](https://stack-planner-a0b8c.web.app)** and add it to your Home Screen. Sign in with the same account and your tasks match on every device.
